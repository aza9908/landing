What is vibe coding

Build apps by describing your idea in words

Vibe coding is an approach to software development where you describe the task in plain language and AI writes the code. Instead of typing code by hand, you set the task, check the result and refine it. On the course we work with Claude: we build a first project, turn it into a working tool in VS Code, publish it online and connect it to data.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No, you don't need any programming experience. You describe the task in plain words and Claude builds the tool. On the course you learn to set the task, check the result and take your project all the way to publication.

How long is the course and where does it take place?

4 sessions over 3 weeks, about 6 hours in total. Sessions 1 and 2 take place in person in Astana on the same day; sessions 3 and 4 are online. We'll announce the date and venue separately.

What do I need to take part?

A laptop on which you can install software, Gmail and GitHub accounts, VS Code with the Claude Code extension, and a Claude Pro subscription at $20/month. Every setup step comes with a video guide in the LMS (in Russian), and we check that everyone is ready at the start of the first session. Step-by-step guide: “How to Set Up Claude Code in VS Code”.

What happens between sessions?

You work on your own project, with a video guide in the LMS (in Russian) for every step: setup, AIRL skills, API, the mini data warehouse and MCP. Between sessions, no participant is left on their own.

How big is the group?

Up to 15 people. Two trainers work with the group on the in-person day.

What will I have at the end?

Your own project, published at a live link and connected to your data, plus a three-step plan for the following week.
