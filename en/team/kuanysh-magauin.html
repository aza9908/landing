About AI Research Labs

AI Research Labs is an applied AI center in Kazakhstan. We teach corporate teams, professionals, and students to work with artificial intelligence in practice, run vibe coding intensives, and build applied AI labs at universities. Our trainers are industry practitioners who work with AI on real projects every day.
